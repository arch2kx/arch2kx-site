Privacy Policy

SeiaSinger Fork

SeiaSinger Fork does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

  • Audio captured via the tabCapture permission is processed locally in the browser and is never recorded, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • Volume and loudness preferences are saved locally using the browser's storage API and remain on the user's device.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used.
