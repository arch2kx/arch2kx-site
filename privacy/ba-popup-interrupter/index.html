Privacy Policy

BA Pop-Up Interrupter

BA Pop-Up Interrupter does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

  • Popup settings (interval, character selection, volume, mute, do-not-disturb hours, site blacklist) are saved locally using the browser's storage API and remain on the user's device.
  • The URL of the active tab is checked in memory against the user's do-not-disturb/blacklist settings to decide whether to show a popup; it is never logged, stored, or sent anywhere.
  • No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used. The extension makes no network requests.
